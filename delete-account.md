# Delete your {{ site.title }} account

## In the app

1. Open {{ site.title }}.
2. Swipe up from the bottom to open the menu.
3. Tap the gear to open **Settings**.
4. Tap **Delete my data** and confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted from our servers at once. The app then clears your history, settings and saved login from the phone and starts over as a fresh install.

## Without the app

Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}?subject=Delete%20my%203%20Seconds%20account">{{ site.contact_email }}</a> with the subject "Delete my 3 Seconds account". Include your handle, as shown in Settings under Account. If you signed in with Apple or Google, also include the email address on that account. We delete the account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

## What is deleted

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Every game attempt on the account | Deleted |
| Your handle and user ID | Deleted |
| The devices linked to the account | Deleted |
| Your Apple or Google sign-in link | Deleted, and the Apple sign-in token is revoked with Apple |
| Saved login on the phone (deletion in the app only) | Deleted |
| History and settings on the phone (deletion in the app only) | Deleted |

Nothing is kept except copies in our hosting provider's rolling backups. Those expire within 30 days and are never used to restore a deleted account.

If you request deletion by email, the copy on your phone stays until you delete the app.
